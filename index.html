Who We Are

The Strait
Difference

Strait Arrow was built on one simple promise: do the job right, every time. We're a Wake County–based general contracting firm specializing in residential renovations — the kind of work that transforms how you live in your home.

What sets us apart? Katy Traversi brings a rare combination to the table: NC Licensed General Contractor, licensed real estate broker, and practicing attorney. That means every project is managed with the accountability and attention to detail your investment deserves.

We're not just builders — we're advocates for our clients from the first call to the final walkthrough.
